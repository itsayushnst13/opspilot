# OpsPilot data provenance

The operational catalog and workflow fixtures in this directory are synthetic and generated by `python scripts/generate_data.py` with seed 42. This includes supplier identities, supplier prices, manufacturing costs, specifications, margins, shipping rates, historical quotes, RFQs, COAs and export-document scenarios.

Public-source chemical identity references are isolated in `verified/chemical_identity.csv`. They contain only identity-level facts from PubChem (preferred name, CAS, molecular formula and molecular weight). They are not manufacturer grade specifications, supplier quotations, freight tariffs, or batch COAs, and must not be used as pricing or quality acceptance authority.

See `provenance_manifest.json` for the machine-readable boundary. The generator does not delete or overwrite the `verified/` directory.
